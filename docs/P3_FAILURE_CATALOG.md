# P3 failure catalog

The exact status and errors below are deterministic Python results recorded in `core/src/test/resources/parity/errors.json`. Missing-information and semantic-coverage results are included for context; coverage warnings are not failures. Natural-language triggers for internal invariants have not been established and are not invented.

| Category | status / errors | Trigger query | UI title and explanation | Correction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unknown destination | `error` / `["UNSUPPORTED_CITY_DATA"]` | `Plan a 2-day trip to wonderland for 1 traveler under $500 total.` | **We couldn't find “wonderland”**. This offline build covers 12 US cities, and this destination isn't one of them. | Check spelling or choose a city; rerun. |
| No candidates | `error` / `["search_hotels: NO_RESULTS"]` | `Plan a 3-day trip to Boston for 1 traveler. Hotel spending must stay under $1 total.` | **No matching options found**. The offline dataset has no options matching the request. | Relax the budget or other constraints; rerun. |
| Budget after repair | `error` / `["Hard budget exceeded"]` | `Please build a three-day trip to Austin for one traveler. My total budget is $1.` | **Couldn't fit the budget**. The least-cost repair still exceeds the budget. | Increase the budget or shorten the trip; rerun. |
| Hotel budget after repair | `error` / `["Hotel budget exceeded"]` | No natural-language trigger confirmed; hotel filtering may return `NO_RESULTS` first. | **Couldn't fit the budget**. The least-cost repair still exceeds the budget. | Increase the budget or shorten the trip; rerun. |
| Internal error | `error` / one of `Planning did not produce an itinerary and budget`, `Itinerary day numbering or duration is inconsistent`, `Daily cost does not match activities`, `Itinerary cost does not match budget calculation` | No natural-language trigger confirmed; these are internal consistency checks. | **Something in this request couldn't be planned offline**. An internal planning check did not complete. | Edit the request and rerun. |
| Missing requirements | `needs_clarification` / `[]` | `Plan a trip to Boston.` (duration); `Plan a 3-day trip for 1 traveler.` (destination) | The clarification question identifies missing destination and/or duration. | Add the missing information and rerun. |

`search_attractions`, `search_hotels`, `search_restaurants`, and `search_transport` can each report `NO_RESULTS`. Only the hotel example above is verified. Coverage reasons `UNRESOLVED_REQUIREMENT_CLAUSE`, `AMBIGUOUS_SCOPE`, `MONETARY_SEMANTIC_GAP`, `CORRECTION_TARGET_UNRESOLVED`, `UNREPRESENTED_NEGATION`, and `TRADEOFF_LANGUAGE` are not errors; Python examples are in `core/src/test/resources/parity/errors.json` and the 200 requirements fixtures. Warnings describe mock estimates, quoting assumptions, repeated attractions, unchecked constraints and dates, repair, absent traveler counts, currency/scope limitations, and budget comparison. They can occur on successful plans and must not be presented as failure errors.
