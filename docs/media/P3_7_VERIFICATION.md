# P3.7 device verification

- Device: iQOO 15; debug build, home screen, system animator duration scale 1.0, light and dark themes.
- Static mode: set Android animator duration scale to 0 and captured two home screenshots three seconds apart after the view settled. The background region was pixel-identical. The scale was restored to 1.0 afterward.
- GPU rendering profile: enabled `debug.hwui.profile=visual_bars`, restarted the app, reset `dumpsys gfxinfo`, then left the home screen visible for 12 seconds. The capture (`p37-gpu.png`) shows the observed bars below the red threshold. `p37-gpu-framestats.txt` records 735 frames, 50th/90th/95th/99th frame-duration percentiles of 9/11/11/11 ms, no frames in the 16 ms or higher histogram buckets, and 0 modern deadline misses. It also reports 425 legacy janky frames (57.82%); that separate high-refresh deadline statistic is not a 16 ms measurement and should not be ignored. An earlier, pre-optimization sample reported a 23 ms median; results can vary with device state, so this observation is not a guarantee for every run. The profiling property was restored to its previous empty value afterward.
- Contrast: title and subtitle paint the theme background behind their text so route elements cannot appear behind glyphs. Token contrast ratios are approximately 5.96:1 for light muted text and 9.53:1 for dark muted text; both themes are covered by `ContrastTest`. The screenshots show the final layouts.
- Motion: `p37-routes.mp4` is the eight-second device recording. The GIF has 80 frames at 10 fps; route-region frames at 0 and 4 seconds differ.
