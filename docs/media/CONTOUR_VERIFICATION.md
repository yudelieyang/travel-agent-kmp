# P3.7 contour-background device verification

- Device: iQOO 15, debug build. `contour-home-*`, `contour-run-*`, and `contour-result-*` contain light and dark captures; cards remain opaque and the same field is visible between them.
- `contour-flow.mp4` is a 9.977-second device recording from home through a preset to the result. The GIF is 100 frames sampled at 10 fps. The background is owned once by the app root and the recording shows the field across navigation.
- Reduced motion: changed Android animator duration scale from 1 to 0 while the result page was visible. Screenshots four seconds apart had an identical 1440-by-300-pixel background strip; `contour-static.png` records the result. Restored the original 1.0 scale.
- GPU profile: enabled `debug.hwui.profile=visual_bars` and observed each page for 12 seconds after resetting gfxinfo. The screenshots show no sustained bars above the 16 ms line. Home: 734 frames, median / 95th / 99th percentile 8 / 9 / 9 ms, zero frames in histogram buckets at or above 16 ms, 0 modern janky frames and 5 legacy janky frames (0.68%). Result: 733 frames, 8 / 9 / 10 ms, zero frames in buckets at or above 16 ms, 0 modern janky frames and 14 legacy janky frames (1.91%). Raw `contour-gpu-home.txt` and `contour-gpu-result.txt` and captures are included. The profile property was restored to its original empty value. An earlier uncached version measured 22–23 ms median, motivating the cached graphics-layer translation; the listed numbers are from the optimized build and do not guarantee every device state.
- Contrast: the six page screenshots were inspected. Light theme text colors were darkened to maintain readability against the index contour. Automated theme tests verify direct text, muted text, primary, error, success, and warning against the strongest contour composited on the background, in both themes; minimum measured token ratio is approximately 5.21:1 light and 5.55:1 dark. Actual anti-aliased screenshot pixels vary.
- Tiling coverage was also inspected after temporarily setting the animator duration scale to 0.02; `contour-fast.png` captures a shifted phase. The scale was restored to 1.0.
